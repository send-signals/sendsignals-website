Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Who controls your data: For pupil check-in data, your school or trust is the data controller and Send Signals Ltd is the data processor, acting only on the school's documented instructions under a UK GDPR (Article 28) Data Processing Agreement. For enquiries you send us through this website (your name, work email, school and role), Send Signals Ltd is the controller of those contact details.

1. What We Collect

We collect the minimum data necessary to provide our service:

  • Student name and the identifier their school already uses for them. Depending on what the school turns on, a pupil signs in with a login card, MyLogin, or a school Microsoft, Google or Apple account. Many pupils never need an email address at all.
  • Lesson check-in responses (green/amber/red)
  • Pre-set message selections
  • Timestamps of interactions
  • Device push-notification token — only if you turn notifications on, used to deliver check-in reminders and alerts

Where pupil and staff details come from: your school chooses how to set us up. If it connects its management information system (MIS) through Wonde, a school data service, we receive from that system: pupil names, dates of birth, unique pupil numbers, year groups, classes and timetables, attendance marks, SEND status and need type, and pupil premium and EAL indicators; and staff names, work email addresses and employment details. We only receive the data sets the school approves in Wonde. Schools that do not use Wonde can upload the same kind of details from an export of their MIS instead.

When you request a demo or contact us through this website, we also collect the details you provide (your name, work email, school and role) so we can respond to your enquiry.

2. How We Use It

Pupil check-in data is used solely to support student wellbeing and SEND provision within the student's school. We never sell pupil data, use it for advertising, or share a pupil's data outside their school's SEND team. Demo-enquiry details are used only to respond to your enquiry — never for advertising, and never sold.

3. Children's Data

Our service is designed around the principles of the ICO Age Appropriate Design Code. Data minimisation is a core principle. Students never see other students' data. All data is processed with the child's best interests as the primary consideration.

4. Data Storage

Pupil data is stored at rest in the UK (Supabase, London region). The application is served over Vercel's global edge network, which is not pinned to a single region. Some sub-processors (email, push notifications, billing and bot-protection) operate outside the UK under appropriate UK transfer safeguards. We use Supabase with Row-Level Security to enforce data isolation at the database level. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

5. Service Providers

We don't sell data, use it for advertising, or allow third-party tracking. To run the service we use a small number of trusted data processors, acting only on our instructions: Supabase (UK-region database), Vercel (application hosting) and Cloudflare (bot protection), Resend (transactional and enquiry email), Stripe (billing — school-facing, no pupil data), Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (delivering push notifications to the app), and — where a school enables it — Anthropic (an optional support assistant for school staff; no pupil data). Each processes data only to deliver our service.

Where a school connects its MIS through Wonde, Wonde passes the school's data to us. Wonde is appointed by the school under its own data processing agreement with the school and acts for the school, not for us, so it is not one of our sub-processors.

6. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, and port your data. For pupil data, the school is the data controller — requests about a pupil's data should go to the school, and we assist the school in responding; if you contact us directly at privacy@sendsignals.co.uk we will pass your request to the relevant school promptly. For details you have given us directly (for example a demo enquiry), contact privacy@sendsignals.co.uk.
