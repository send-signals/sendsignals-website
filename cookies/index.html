Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. What Cookies We Use

We use only essential cookies required for the platform to function: authentication session tokens and security cookies. On our public sign-up and demo forms we also use Cloudflare Turnstile, which sets a short-lived challenge cookie to block automated abuse. We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies.

2. No Third-Party Tracking

We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any third-party tracking. Your browsing is not monitored or profiled.

3. Your Choice

Essential cookies are necessary for the service to work. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but this may prevent the platform from functioning correctly.

For any questions about cookies, contact: hello@sendsignals.co.uk
